About Me.

Hello everyone! I’m Ramsha Tariq, a passionate artist from Pakistan deeply inspired by my mother, who is also a talented artist. While my primary focus is on oil painting, I am currently expanding my expertise to include acrylics. Through my artwork, I strive to evoke a sense of nostalgia, forging meaningful connections between viewers and their cherished memories and emotions.

If you are interested in purchasing any artwork or have any query, please contact me on WhatsApp, Instagram or email.
